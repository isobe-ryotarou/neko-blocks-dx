NEKO BLOCKS DX 4.1 - Audio Sources

The game streams audio from Wikimedia Commons. The audio files are not bundled in this ZIP.
Playback is accelerated modestly while preserving pitch when the browser supports it.

STAGE 1 - Beethoven: Ode to Joy
Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ode_to_Joy.ogg

STAGE 2 - Mozart: Eine kleine Nachtmusik, K.525, 1st movement
Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mozart_K525_Serenade_in_G_Major_1_-_Allegro.ogg

STAGE 3 - Mozart: Rondo Alla Turca / Turkish March
Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rondo_Alla_Turka.ogg
Commons identifies this file as free of known copyright restrictions / Public Domain Mark.

STAGE 4 - Offenbach: Orpheus in the Underworld - Can Can section
Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Offenbach_-_Orpheus_in_the_Underworld_-_Overture,_Can_Can_section.ogg
Commons states that this recording was explicitly dedicated to the public domain.

STAGE 5 - Rossini: William Tell Overture (second half)
Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:William_Tell2.ogg
Commons lists the recording as Public Domain.

Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Brahms_nikisch_hd5.ogg
Historic 1906 performance; Commons marks the work public domain.

STAGE 7 - Rimsky-Korsakov: Flight of the Bumblebee
Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rimsky-Korsakov_-_flight_of_the_bumblebee.oga

STAGE 8 - Grieg: In the Hall of the Mountain King
Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Musopen_-_In_the_Hall_Of_The_Mountain_King.ogg

STAGE 9 - Beethoven: Symphony No.5, 1st movement

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ludwig_van_Beethoven_-_symphony_no._5_in_c_minor,_op._67_-_i._allegro_con_brio.ogg

STAGE 10 - Rimsky-Korsakov: Flight of the Bumblebee - FINAL RUSH
Same public-domain U.S. Army Band recording as Stage 7, played faster.
