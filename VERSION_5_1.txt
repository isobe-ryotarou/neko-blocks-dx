NEKO BLOCKS DX 5.1 - COLOR CHAIN REBUILD

Core rules:
- Each tetromino still has four cells, but every cell has its own color.
- 4 or more orthogonally connected cells of the same color disappear.
- Diagonal-only connections do not count.
- After a clear, columns collapse downward.
- The board is checked again after gravity; this can create chains.
- One clear-resolution event increments CLEAR by 1.
- Every 10 CLEAR advances one stage.
- Stage 10 begins at CLEAR 90.
- 100 CLEAR remains the long-term completion target conceptually.
- Stage transitions are separated from board resolution state.
